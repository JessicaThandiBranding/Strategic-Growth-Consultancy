About

More than marketing.
A strategic partner for your next stage of growth

I’m Jessica, and I work with ambitious founders and service-led businesses to bring clarity, focus and momentum to their next stage of growth. With a background across marketing, business development, client experience, events and brand strategy, I take a holistic view of what makes a business work, from the bigger picture to the details that shape how it’s experienced.

I’m collaborative, commercial and hands-on, and I care about creating solutions that are practical, tailored and aligned with your goals. Whether you need a focused review, strategic guidance or hands-on support with a defined project, I’ll work alongside you to understand what you need, set clear priorities and help you move forward with confidence.

My approach is built on clarity, intention and alignment, so you can grow in a way that feels right for you, your business and the clients you serve.

Rooted in Cape Town. Focused on your growth.

  • Value proposition

Review the journey from first enquiry to follow-up, identifying friction in communication, onboarding and service delivery. Recommendations focus on making the experience easier for customers and more manageable for your business.

  • Communication strategy

Marketing & Content Support

  • Social media guidance
  • Campaign planning

Events & Brand Activations

My Approach

Clear thinking. Beautiful execution.Practical growth.

A business can offer an excellent service and still find that its messaging, website or customer journey falls short of the experience it wants to deliver. I help founders and service-led businesses examine those gaps and decide what to address first.

My work draws on experience across marketing, business development, client experience, events and brand strategy, with attention to both the bigger picture and the details customers notice. Together, we agree the priorities and a scope that fits your business, so you know what the work involves and how to move forward.

  1. Understand

    We begin with your business, your customers, your challenges and what you want to achieve.

  2. Assess
